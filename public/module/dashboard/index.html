How Your Files Are Protected
Understanding our military-grade security model.

When you upload a file to Famous Storage, your browser instantly generates a unique 256-bit AES encryption key. Your file is then locked (encrypted) using this key before it ever leaves your device.

We only store the locked, scrambled version of your file on our servers. The unique key used to lock it is downloaded straight to your computer as a .txt file.

Because we never receive your key, it is mathematically impossible for anyone at Famous Storage (or any hackers who breach our servers) to view your files.

⚠️
What You MUST Keep Secret
Crucial instructions for maintaining your privacy.
  • The Downloaded Key Files: Every time you upload or share a file, you receive an AES decryption key. Do NOT share this key publicly unless you want the world to be able to decrypt and view that specific file.
  • If you lose a key: Because we do not store your keys, if you lose the key file for an encrypted document, that document is lost forever. We cannot recover it for you.
  • Sharing Links: Only send your files and decryption keys to trusted individuals via secure, private channels.
