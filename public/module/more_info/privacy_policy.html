Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

At Famous Storage, your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal and organizational data.

2. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

We employ a strict zero-knowledge security model for your files. All files uploaded to Famous Storage are encrypted client-side using AES-256 before transmission. We do not have the keys to decrypt your files. Only you and the parties you explicitly share your keys with can access the raw data.

3. Information We Collect

While we cannot see your files, we do collect necessary operational data:

  • Account Information: Email addresses, names, and authentication tokens (e.g., Google OAuth data).
  • Usage Metadata: File sizes, upload timestamps, and storage quotas to manage infrastructure limits.
  • Analytics: Anonymous dashboard performance metrics to improve the platform experience.

4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide and maintain the Famous Storage platform. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties under any circumstances.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data. When you delete a file from the Famous Storage dashboard, it is permanently purged from our servers and storage buckets. If you wish to delete your entire account, all associated data is permanently erased.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our security practices, please contact our support team.
